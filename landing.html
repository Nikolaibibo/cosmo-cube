The journey

Three worlds, one warp gate each

Every level is a little harder than the last, but always fair: checkpoints in the middle, generous hitboxes, and no power-up you can't play without.

Level 1

Nebula Fields

A gentle start under purple skies. Learn to run, jump, duck and shoot, then ride your first moving platform.

Level 2

Asteroid Belt

Wider chasms, more monsters, crumbling rocks, and anti-gravity bubbles that turn every jump into a slow, floaty leap.

Level 3

Crystal Moon

Everything at once, and at the end the Nebula Jelly: fireball fans, falling stones, ten hits to win.

Know your universe

Cute. Spooky. Stompable.

Shoot them with W or jump on their heads. Collect power-ups to jump higher or survive a hit.

How to play

Easy to learn, keyboard only

Gamepads work too. Collect coins in quick succession to build a combo of up to ×8, and earn up to three stars per level.
